SLIDE 2: MARKET CONTEXT
================================================================================

Industry Trends:
- Enterprise software M&A activity up 34% YoY in 2023
- Median EV/Revenue multiple for SaaS companies: 8.2x (down from 12.4x in 2021)
- Median EV/EBITDA multiple for profitable SaaS: 22.5x
- Private equity dry powder at record $2.6 trillion

Aurora's Position:
- #3 market share in enterprise data analytics (est. 8.2% market share)
- Market leader: Palantir (18.4%), #2: Snowflake (12.1%)
- Strong customer retention: 118% Net Revenue Retention
- Accelerating SaaS transition: SaaS mix grew from 38.3% to 41.9% of revenue

Competitive Risks:
- AI-native competitors gaining traction (Databricks, DataRobot)
- Customer concentration: Top 10 customers represent 28% of revenue
- Key person dependency: CTO Dr. James Wu holds 12 critical patents

================================================================================
SLIDE 3: PRELIMINARY INDICATIONS OF INTEREST
================================================================================

Three parties submitted non-binding indications:

Party A — Vertex Capital Partners (Private Equity)
  - Offer: $55 - $60 per share (cash)
  - Financing: Committed debt financing from JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs
  - Timeline: 60-day exclusivity, 90-day to signing
  - Due Diligence: Standard, no unusual conditions
  - Certainty: HIGH — experienced buyer, financing secured

Party B — Meridian Software Corp (Strategic)
  - Offer: $50 - $54 per share (60% cash / 40% stock)
  - Synergies: Estimated $45M annual cost synergies by Year 3
  - Timeline: 90-day exclusivity, 120-day to signing
  - Due Diligence: Extensive technology DD required
  - Antitrust: MODERATE RISK — overlapping customer base (~15%)
  - Certainty: MEDIUM — antitrust review could delay/block

Party C — Atlas Data Holdings (Private Equity Consortium)
  - Offer: $48 - $52 per share (cash)
  - Financing: Partially committed, ~30% equity shortfall
  - Timeline: 90-day exclusivity, 150-day to signing
  - Due Diligence: Extensive, including management presentations
  - Certainty: LOW — financing gap, complex consortium structure

SLIDE 5: MANAGEMENT RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================

The Management Team and Goldman Sachs recommend:

1. PROCEED with Vertex Capital Partners as the preferred bidder
   - Highest offer range ($55-$60)
   - All-cash consideration — no stock price risk for shareholders
   - High certainty of close — committed financing
   - Shortest timeline to signing

2. MAINTAIN engagement with Meridian Software as a backup bidder
   - Could drive price improvement through competition
   - Synergy value could justify higher price if antitrust resolved

3. DECLINE Atlas Data Holdings
   - Lowest offer range
   - Uncommitted financing creates execution risk
   - Complex consortium structure

BOARD RESOLUTION
================================================================================

RESOLVED, that the Board of Directors hereby authorizes the Company to:
(1) Grant Vertex Capital Partners a 60-day exclusivity period;
(2) Open the virtual data room for Vertex's due diligence team;
(3) Retain Skadden Arps to prepare the definitive merger agreement;
(4) Authorize management to negotiate within a price floor of $55.00 per share.

Vote: 7-0 (unanimous)
