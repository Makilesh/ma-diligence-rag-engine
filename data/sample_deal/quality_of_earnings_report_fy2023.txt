SECTION 1: SCOPE AND BASIS OF PRESENTATION
================================================================================

This report presents a quality of earnings analysis of Aurora Technologies Inc.
(the "Company") for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2022 and December 31,
2023. Our procedures included analysis of revenue recognition policies, review
of accrual completeness, examination of non-recurring items, and normalization
of reported EBITDA.

This is not an audit. The Company's statutory financial statements were audited
by Deloitte & Touche LLP, which issued an unqualified opinion for both periods.

SECTION 3: NOTES TO NORMALIZATION ADJUSTMENTS
================================================================================

Note 1 - Restructuring Charges ($4.5M, FY2023)
    Severance and facility exit costs associated with the closure of the Austin
    development center in Q3 FY2023. 32 employees were affected. Management
    represents this program is complete and no further charges are expected.
    We consider this adjustment appropriate and non-recurring.

Note 2 - Non-recurring Legal Fees ($2.8M, FY2023)
    External counsel fees relating to the DataFlow Systems patent litigation
    (Case No. 2023-CV-04821). We note that this matter remains unresolved and
    that future periods may incur comparable fees. Buyer should treat this
    add-back with caution: it is non-recurring only if the litigation concludes.
    We have flagged this as a QUALIFIED ADJUSTMENT.

Note 3 - SOC 2 Remediation ($1.6M, FY2023)
    One-time external consulting and tooling spend to remediate control gaps
    identified in the FY2022 SOC 2 Type II examination. The FY2023 examination
    was issued with no exceptions. Adjustment accepted.

Note 4 - Founder Compensation Above Market ($1.9M, both years)
    CEO Elena Marsh and CTO Dr. James Wu receive aggregate cash compensation
    approximately $1.9 million above the 75th percentile of the peer benchmark
    set. Post-closing employment terms are addressed in the retention agreements
    described in the employment agreement schedule. Adjustment accepted.

Note 5 - Capitalized Software Adjustment (($3.2M), FY2023)
    The Company capitalizes internally developed software over a 3-year life.
    Peer practice in this segment is predominantly to expense. We have reduced
    EBITDA by the amount capitalized in each period to present a comparable
    figure. This is a DOWNWARD adjustment and reduces Adjusted EBITDA.

Note 6 - Deferred Revenue Haircut Reversal (($1.4M), FY2023)
    Purchase accounting will require a fair value haircut to acquired deferred
    revenue, estimated at $1.4 million. This reduces post-closing recognized
    revenue and is presented here so the Buyer models the correct run-rate.

SECTION 6: KEY OBSERVATIONS FOR THE BUYER
================================================================================

1. Adjusted EBITDA of $99.0 million exceeds reported EBITDA of $92.8 million by
   $6.2 million. A valuation struck on Adjusted EBITDA rather than reported
   EBITDA produces a materially lower implied multiple.

2. The DataFlow legal fee add-back ($2.8M) is qualified. If the litigation
   continues into FY2024, Adjusted EBITDA is overstated by that amount.

3. Perpetual license revenue is in structural decline and should be excluded
   from run-rate assumptions.

4. Founder compensation normalization assumes both founders remain. If either
   departs, replacement cost at market may exceed the $1.9 million add-back.

5. The capitalized software adjustment is the single largest downward
   normalization and reflects a genuine accounting policy difference, not an
   earnings quality concern.
