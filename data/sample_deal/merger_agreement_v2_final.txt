AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER

This Agreement and Plan of Merger (this "Agreement") is entered into as of
March 15, 2024, by and among:

  VERTEX CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Buyer"),
  AURORA TECHNOLOGIES INC., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and
  AURORA MERGER SUB INC., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary
  of Buyer ("Merger Sub").

================================================================================
ARTICLE I — THE MERGER
================================================================================

Section 1.1 — The Merger
Upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Effective
Time, Merger Sub shall be merged with and into the Company, with the Company
surviving the Merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of Buyer.

Section 1.2 — Consideration
Each share of Company Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to
the Effective Time shall be converted into the right to receive $58.00 per share
in cash (the "Per Share Merger Consideration"), without interest thereon. This
represents a premium of approximately 42% to the Company's 90-day volume-weighted
average closing price of $40.85 as of March 14, 2024.

The aggregate Merger Consideration is approximately $696 million, subject to
customary adjustments as set forth in Section 2.4.

Section 1.3 — Treatment of Company Stock Options and RSUs
All outstanding unvested Company stock options and restricted stock units shall
fully vest and be cancelled at the Effective Time in exchange for a cash payment
equal to the Per Share Merger Consideration less the applicable exercise price
(for options) or the full Per Share Merger Consideration (for RSUs).

================================================================================
ARTICLE III — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF THE COMPANY
================================================================================

Section 3.5 — Financial Statements
The Company has delivered to Buyer (a) the audited consolidated financial
statements for FY2022 and FY2023, (b) the unaudited quarterly financial
statements for Q1 2024 ended March 31, 2024. Such financial statements:
(i) were prepared in accordance with GAAP applied on a consistent basis,
(ii) fairly present in all material respects the consolidated financial
position and results of operations of the Company for the periods covered,
and (iii) are consistent with the books and records of the Company.

Section 3.8 — Material Contracts
Schedule 3.8 sets forth a complete list of all Material Contracts. As used
herein, "Material Contracts" means any contract involving annual consideration
in excess of $5.0 million or having a term longer than three (3) years. The
Company is not in breach or default under any Material Contract.

Section 3.12 — Intellectual Property
The Company owns or has valid licenses to all Intellectual Property necessary
for the conduct of its business. No claims are pending or, to the Company's
Knowledge, threatened alleging that the Company infringes any third-party
Intellectual Property. The Company holds 47 issued U.S. patents and 23 pending
patent applications.

Section 3.15 — Litigation
Except as set forth in Schedule 3.15:
(a) There is no Action pending or, to the Company's Knowledge, threatened
    against the Company that would reasonably be expected to result in
    liability in excess of $2.0 million.
(b) The Company is not subject to any outstanding order, decree, or judgment.
(c) As of the date hereof, the Company is a defendant in the matter of
    DataFlow Systems Inc. v. Aurora Technologies Inc. (Case No. 2023-CV-04821,
    U.S. District Court, Northern District of California), alleging patent
    infringement related to certain data processing algorithms. The Company
    believes these claims are without merit and intends to vigorously defend.
    The Company's outside counsel has assessed the probable exposure at
    $3.5 million to $8.0 million.

================================================================================
ARTICLE V — COVENANTS
================================================================================

Section 5.1 — Conduct of Business Pending the Merger
During the period from the date of this Agreement until the Effective Time, the
Company shall conduct its business in the ordinary course and shall not, without
the prior written consent of Buyer:

(a) amend its certificate of incorporation or bylaws;
(b) issue, sell, or grant any shares of capital stock or equity securities;
(c) declare or pay any dividends;
(d) incur any indebtedness in excess of $10.0 million;
(e) make capital expenditures in excess of $5.0 million individually or
    $15.0 million in the aggregate;
(f) hire any employee with annual base compensation in excess of $250,000;
(g) terminate or materially amend any Material Contract;
(h) settle any litigation in excess of $1.0 million.

Section 5.3 — No-Shop Provision
From the date of this Agreement until the earlier of the Effective Time or
termination of this Agreement, the Company shall not, and shall cause its
representatives not to, directly or indirectly: (a) solicit, initiate, or
knowingly facilitate any Acquisition Proposal; (b) enter into any agreement
regarding an Acquisition Proposal; or (c) provide non-public information to
any third party in connection with an Acquisition Proposal.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Company may engage in discussions with a
third party making an unsolicited Acquisition Proposal that the Board determines
in good faith (after consultation with outside counsel and financial advisors)
constitutes or could reasonably be expected to lead to a Superior Proposal.

================================================================================
ARTICLE VII — TERMINATION
================================================================================

Section 7.1 — Termination
This Agreement may be terminated at any time prior to the Effective Time:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and the Company;
(b) by either party if the Merger has not been consummated by September 15, 2024
    (the "Outside Date");
(c) by Buyer if there has been an uncured breach by the Company of any
    representation, warranty, or covenant;
(d) by the Company to accept a Superior Proposal, subject to the payment of
    the Termination Fee.

Section 7.2 — Termination Fee
If this Agreement is terminated pursuant to Section 7.1(d) (Superior Proposal),
the Company shall pay Buyer a termination fee of $20.88 million (representing
3.0% of the aggregate Merger Consideration) (the "Termination Fee"). If this
Agreement is terminated by Buyer pursuant to Section 7.1(c) (Company Breach),
the Company shall pay Buyer a reverse termination fee of $13.92 million
(representing 2.0% of the aggregate Merger Consideration).

================================================================================
ARTICLE VIII — INDEMNIFICATION
================================================================================

Section 8.1 — Survival of Representations
The representations and warranties of the Company shall survive the Closing
for a period of eighteen (18) months, except that Fundamental Representations
(Sections 3.1, 3.2, 3.5, 3.12) shall survive for thirty-six (36) months.

Section 8.2 — Indemnification Cap
The aggregate liability of the Company's stockholders for indemnification
claims under this Article VIII shall not exceed $69.6 million (representing
10% of the aggregate Merger Consideration) (the "General Cap"). For
Fundamental Representations, the cap shall be $174.0 million (25% of the
aggregate Merger Consideration).

Section 8.3 — Deductible
The Company's stockholders shall not be liable for indemnification claims
until the aggregate amount of Losses exceeds $3.48 million (representing
0.5% of the aggregate Merger Consideration) (the "Deductible"), after which
the Company's stockholders shall be liable for all Losses from the first dollar.

================================================================================
ARTICLE IX — CHANGE OF CONTROL PROVISIONS
================================================================================

Section 9.1 — Change of Control Definition
For purposes of this Agreement, a "Change of Control" shall be deemed to have
occurred upon consummation of the Merger.

Section 9.2 — Effect on Material Contracts
The Company has identified the following Material Contracts that contain change
of control provisions requiring consent or providing termination rights:
(a) Master Services Agreement with Northstar Defense Systems (annual value
    $12.4M) — requires 60-day notice and consent.
(b) Technology License Agreement with Pacific Data Corp (annual value $8.7M)
    — counterparty has right to terminate within 90 days of Change of Control.
(c) Cloud Infrastructure Agreement with Amazon Web Services (annual value
    $6.2M) — no change of control provision.
