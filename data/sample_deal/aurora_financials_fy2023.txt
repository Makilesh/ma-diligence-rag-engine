Note 7 — Debt:
The Company maintains a $150M senior secured revolving credit facility with
JPMorgan Chase, of which $110M was drawn as of December 31, 2023. The facility
matures on June 30, 2026, and bears interest at SOFR + 225bps. The facility
contains financial covenants requiring: (i) Net Debt/EBITDA ≤ 3.5x, and
(ii) Interest Coverage Ratio ≥ 3.0x. As of December 31, 2023, the Company
was in compliance with all covenants.

Note 12 — Subsequent Events:
On February 15, 2024, the Company's Board of Directors approved the exploration
of strategic alternatives, including a potential sale of the Company. The Company
has retained Goldman Sachs as financial advisor.
