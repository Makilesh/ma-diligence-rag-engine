SECTION 3: MOST FAVORED NATION AND PRICING PROVISIONS
================================================================================

Northstar Financial Group holds a most favored nation ("MFN") pricing clause
entitling it to the lowest per-seat rate offered to any customer of comparable
volume. Management represents the MFN has not been triggered. Buyer diligence
identified one FY2023 arrangement (Continental Retail Group) priced 4% below the
Northstar rate at a lower volume tier; counsel has advised that the volume tiers
are not comparable and the MFN is therefore not triggered. This position has not
been tested with the customer.

Pacific Data Systems holds a price protection clause capping annual increases at
3% through the contract term ending June 30, 2025.

SECTION 5: SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENTS AND PENALTIES
================================================================================

Northstar Financial Group and Meridian Health Networks contracts include
uptime service level agreements of 99.95% measured monthly, with service
credits of up to 15% of monthly fees for sustained breach.

During FY2023 the Company recorded $0.6 million in service credits, all
attributable to a single incident in August 2023 affecting the Meridian Health
Networks environment. No customer exercised a termination right as a result.

Meridian Health Networks is subject to HIPAA. The Company executed a Business
Associate Agreement dated April 12, 2022. Buyer's regulatory counsel should
confirm the BAA survives the transaction.
