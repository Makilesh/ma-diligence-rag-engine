SECTION 4: CHANGE OF CONTROL AND THE TRANSACTION
================================================================================

Section 8.1(k) of the Credit Agreement defines an Event of Default to include
any transaction in which any person or group acquires beneficial ownership of
more than 35% of the voting equity of the Company.

The Vertex Capital Partners merger constitutes a change of control within this
definition. On the occurrence of such an Event of Default:

  - All commitments terminate automatically;
  - All outstanding principal, accrued interest and fees become immediately due
    and payable without further notice or demand.

Consequence for the transaction: the full $85 million drawn balance must be
repaid at closing. There is no prepayment penalty, but accrued interest and a
breakage indemnity for any SOFR period terminated mid-period will be payable.
Estimated breakage cost: less than $50,000.

The Company has NOT sought a consent or waiver from the lending syndicate.
Buyer's financing plan assumes the facility is repaid and replaced. The merger
agreement requires delivery of a customary payoff letter and lien releases at
closing as a condition to Buyer's obligation to close.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: NEGATIVE COVENANTS OF NOTE
================================================================================

While the facility remains outstanding, the Company may not, without lender
consent:

  - Incur additional indebtedness in excess of $15 million;
  - Make restricted payments (including dividends) in excess of $10 million per
    fiscal year;
  - Dispose of assets outside the ordinary course with proceeds exceeding
    $20 million in any fiscal year;
  - Permit any subsidiary to guarantee other indebtedness.

None of these covenants restrict the Company's ability to enter into the merger
agreement itself; they restrict operations pending closing. The interim
operating covenants in Article V of the merger agreement are drafted to be
consistent with these restrictions.

SECTION 6: CAPITAL LEASES AND OTHER OBLIGATIONS
================================================================================

Capital lease obligations of $6.4 million relate to data centre equipment under
three master lease schedules maturing between 2025 and 2027. Two of the three
schedules contain change of control provisions requiring lessor consent; the
lessor in each case is the same counterparty and has indicated in writing that
consent will be granted upon customary notice.

Operating lease commitments (not included in debt) total $34.7 million over the
remaining terms, principally the San Jose headquarters lease expiring in 2029.
The Austin facility lease was terminated in Q3 FY2023 as part of the
restructuring; the associated exit cost is included in the $4.5 million
restructuring charge.
