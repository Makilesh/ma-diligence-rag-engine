SECTION 1: ANTITRUST AND MERGER CONTROL
================================================================================

HART-SCOTT-RODINO (UNITED STATES)

The transaction is reportable under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements
Act. The transaction value of approximately $696 million exceeds the size-of-
transaction threshold, and both parties exceed the size-of-person thresholds.

  Filing fee (transactions $500M to $1B tier)              $260,000
  Filing responsibility                                    Both parties
  Fee allocation per merger agreement                      Buyer pays 100%
  Initial waiting period                                   30 calendar days

Filing was submitted on March 20, 2024. The initial waiting period expires on
April 19, 2024 absent a Second Request.

Substantive assessment: Vertex Capital Partners is a financial sponsor with no
existing portfolio company in enterprise data analytics. There is no horizontal
overlap and no vertical relationship. Counsel assesses the probability of a
Second Request as low.

SECTION 2: DATA PRIVACY POSTURE
================================================================================

GDPR (EUROPEAN UNION)

Aurora processes personal data of EU data subjects on behalf of customers and
acts as a processor in that capacity. It acts as a controller with respect to
its own employee and prospect data.

  Standard Contractual Clauses in place                    Yes, 2021 SCCs
  Records of processing (Article 30)                       Maintained
  Data Protection Impact Assessments                       2 completed
  EU representative appointed (Article 27)                 Yes, Dublin
  Data Protection Officer                                  Not required;
                                                           voluntarily appointed
  Supervisory authority inquiries                          None received
  Reportable personal data breaches (last 3 years)         None

A transfer impact assessment was completed in November 2023 following the EU-US
Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision. Aurora is NOT self-certified under the
Data Privacy Framework and relies on SCCs plus supplementary measures.

CALIFORNIA (CCPA/CPRA)

Aurora qualifies as a "service provider" for customer data. Its standard data
processing addendum contains the required service provider terms. Aurora does
not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined.

HIPAA

One customer, Meridian Health Networks, is a covered entity. Aurora executed a
Business Associate Agreement dated April 12, 2022. Aurora's HIPAA compliance
programme was reviewed and no material gaps were identified. The BAA does not
contain an assignment restriction and survives a change of control.

SECURITY INCIDENTS

One security incident occurred during the review period. In August 2023 a
misconfigured storage bucket in a non-production environment was accessible
without authentication for approximately 41 hours. The environment contained
synthetic test data only. No personal data or customer data was exposed. Counsel
confirmed no notification obligation arose under GDPR, CCPA or any state breach
notification statute. The incident is unrelated to the service credit recorded
for the Meridian Health Networks availability event in the same month.

SECTION 4: CONDITIONS TO CLOSING
================================================================================

The regulatory conditions to closing under Article VI of the merger agreement
are:

  1. Expiration or early termination of the HSR waiting period.
  2. No injunction or order prohibiting the transaction.
  3. No law enacted after signing that makes the transaction illegal.

There is no financing condition. There is no condition relating to obtaining
customer consents, notwithstanding that two material customer contracts
(Northstar Financial Group and Pacific Data Systems, together representing
$92.8 million or 20.5% of FY2023 revenue) contain consent-based termination
rights. Buyer therefore bears the risk of those consents not being obtained.

Counsel draws attention to this allocation: the customer consent risk sits with
the Buyer post-closing rather than operating as a condition, and is addressed,
if at all, through the indemnification provisions in Article VIII.

SECTION 5: OUTSIDE DATE AND TERMINATION
================================================================================

The outside date under Section 7.1(b) is December 31, 2024, subject to a single
automatic extension of 90 days if the only unsatisfied condition is the HSR
waiting period.

Given a March 20, 2024 HSR filing and a low assessed probability of a Second
Request, counsel considers the outside date to provide substantial headroom.
Even in a Second Request scenario, typical timelines of 6 to 10 months from
filing would fall within the extended date.
