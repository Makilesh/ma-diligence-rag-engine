SECTION 2: CHANGE OF CONTROL SEVERANCE ("GOLDEN PARACHUTE")
================================================================================

Each named executive officer is party to an employment agreement providing for
severance upon a qualifying termination following a change of control. All five
agreements are "double trigger": severance is payable only if the executive is
terminated without cause, or resigns for good reason, within 24 months after the
change of control.

Severance multiples and estimated cost, assuming all five executives are
terminated immediately following closing:

Name                Multiple    Cash Severance   Equity Acceleration   Total
------------------  ---------   --------------   -------------------   -----------
Elena Marsh          3.0x           $3,187,500            $4,260,000    $7,447,500
Dr. James Wu         2.5x           $2,160,000            $3,595,000    $5,755,000
Robert Chen          2.0x           $1,424,000            $1,543,000    $2,967,000
Priya Raghavan       2.0x           $1,428,000            $1,370,000    $2,798,000
Marcus Delgado       1.5x             $866,250              $777,000    $1,643,250
                                 ------------          ------------   -----------
Total Potential Parachute Cost      $9,065,750           $11,545,000   $20,610,750

The multiple is applied to the sum of base salary and target cash bonus.

Section 280G: Marsh, Wu and Chen are expected to exceed the Section 280G safe
harbour, exposing the Company to a 20% excise tax and loss of the corresponding
deduction. The Company has NOT obtained a shareholder cleansing vote. Estimated
excise tax exposure is $2.4 million. The merger agreement allocates this cost to
the Company at closing.

SECTION 4: KEY PERSON DEPENDENCY
================================================================================

Dr. James Wu, Chief Technology Officer, is named as sole or first inventor on 12
of the Company's 47 issued U.S. patents, including the four patents underlying
the real-time data reconciliation engine that is the subject of the DataFlow
Systems litigation.

Dr. Wu's employment agreement contains:
  - A non-competition covenant of 12 months post-termination, enforceable in
    Delaware but of uncertain enforceability in California, where he resides.
  - A non-solicitation covenant of 24 months.
  - An assignment-of-inventions clause covering all work product.

Dr. Wu has NOT executed a post-closing employment agreement with Vertex Capital
Partners. Buyer has identified this as a material integration risk. Aurora
management represents that Dr. Wu has verbally indicated willingness to remain
for a transition period of not less than 12 months, but this is not documented.

CEO Elena Marsh has indicated she intends to depart at closing. Her severance is
therefore expected to be triggered.

SECTION 5: EQUITY PLAN TREATMENT
================================================================================

At the effective time, each outstanding option and restricted stock unit will be
treated as follows:

  Vested options            Cancelled in exchange for a cash payment equal to
                            the excess of $58.00 over the exercise price,
                            multiplied by the number of shares.

  Unvested options          Accelerated in full and cashed out on the same
                            basis. Total acceleration cost: $11,545,000 across
                            named executives and $4,180,000 for all other
                            employees.

  Restricted stock units    Accelerated in full and settled in cash at $58.00
                            per share.

  Employee Stock Purchase   The current offering period terminates immediately
  Plan                      prior to closing; accumulated payroll deductions are
                            applied to a final purchase.

Total equity plan cost at closing across all holders: $15,725,000.
