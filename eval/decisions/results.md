One annotator (the implementing agent).

Caveats: one annotator; small TEST positive counts (see the rows above), so a difference of one or two chunks moves F1 by several points; the regex fixes were written after reading the corpus, so regex_fixed is optimistic on corpus rows (most visibly for category filenames); synthetic rows were written by the same agent that wrote the questions.
